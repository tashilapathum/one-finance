# One Finance

[![Get it on Google Play](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/7/78/Google_Play_Store_badge_EN.svg)](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.tantalum.onefinance)

**One Finance** is a personal finance app designed for on-the-go use. You can add your daily incomes, expenses, and other transactions with just a few clicks. One Finance is a feature-rich offline logger for your daily transactions.

The features of One Finance are designed to match your real-life experiences. Download and see if it really is!

Or just read the features:

## 💵 **Wallet**

This is the main page of the app. The wallet shows the balance of your physical wallet so you don’t have to check it all the time. You have only one actual wallet, so there are no multiple wallets like in some apps. Instead, there are multiple accounts.

Using the Wallet page, you can:

*  Add transactions with 2 taps
*  Add incomes
*  Add expenses with categories
*  Optionally choose a category
*  Transfer money to your accounts
*  Add expenses with just 1 tap from the Quick List
*  See ‘Today’, ‘This week’ and ‘This month’ reports
*  Tap the balance to edit manually

## 🏦 **Account Manager**

You can add multiple accounts and switch between them. These accounts can be your real-life bank accounts or just for managing different kinds of expenses/incomes.

*  Switch between accounts with 1 tap
*  See all transactions of each account
*  Add payments directly from accounts
*  Transfer money between accounts

## 📃 **Transaction History**

This page lists all your incomes, expenses, deposits, and withdrawals.

*  Search and edit transaction amount, description, category, or date
*  Filter by date: Today, Yesterday, This week, or Last week
*  Filter by type: Income or Expense
*  Filter by category
*  Sort by date or amount

## 📈 **Investments**

Manage and monitor all your investments in one place.

*  Add invested amounts and return values
*  Calculate profit automatically
*  Add special notes
*  See investment history
*  Group similar investments by tags
*  Sort investments by most profitable, return value, invested amount, or longest time period

## ❇️ **Categories**

Categories are optional for quick logging. You can always choose or edit the category later.

*  Add as many categories as you want
*  Choose *any* color
*  Adjust color transparency
*  Use the built-in color picker
*  View spending per category in a pie chart

## 📊 **Reports**

Access Daily, Weekly, and Monthly reports:

*  Total income and expenses with a chart
*  Income vs. expense comparison with previous period
*  Total budget and remaining budget
*  Item with the highest expense
*  View reports from a specific day

## ⚒️ **Tools**

Extra tools to make your life easier:

*  **Shopping Cart**: Jot down your shopping list
*  **Bills**: Track paid and due bills, add to your calendar
*  **Loans**: Track borrowed or lent money
*  **Calculator**: Do basic math quickly
*  **Quick List**: Save common expenses for 1-tap adding

## ⚙️ **Customization**

Personalize One Finance:

*  Dark and light modes
*  Set a PIN
*  Change home page
*  Allow or restrict negative balance

Download the app from [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.tantalum.onefinance)
